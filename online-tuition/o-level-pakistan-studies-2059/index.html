FAQs

O Level Pakistan Studies 2059: Your Questions Answered.

Do you offer one-to-one O Level Pakistan Studies tuition online?

Yes. Valeem teaches Cambridge O Level Pakistan Studies (2059) live and one-to-one online. Your child's teacher holds an A or B grade in Pakistan Studies and has passed our interview and training.

What does O Level Pakistan Studies 2059 tuition cover?

The full Pakistan Studies 2059 syllabus: The history and culture of Pakistan, the environment of pakistan: land, climate, water and natural resources, agriculture, industry and trade and population, transport and development, with past-paper practice and exam technique.

Which O Level Pakistan Studies topics do students find hardest?

Most students we teach need extra help with remembering events in order and writing explained, balanced answers. The teacher finds your child's gaps in the free demo and builds the plan around them.

Do you teach the latest syllabus?

Yes. Teachers follow the Pakistan Studies 2059 syllabus for your child's exam year, including any changes Cambridge has announced, and use past papers from recent series.

How much is O Level Pakistan Studies tuition?

Tuition is a monthly plan. The fee depends on the grade, the number of months, classes per week, class length and whether you want a fixed time slot. A counsellor shares the fee after the free demo.

Can students in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf join?

Yes. About 70% of our families live in the Middle East, most of them in Saudi Arabia. Classes are scheduled after school in your time zone.
