Frequently asked questions

Questions Homeschooling Parents Ask Us.

The programme

What is Valeem Homeschool Pathways?

Valeem Homeschool Pathways is a structured online academic programme designed to support families who have chosen homeschooling for their children.

It combines live online classes, experienced educators, academic learning stages, digital resources, parent guidance and a progression-oriented academic approach.

Is Valeem a conventional school?

No. Valeem Homeschool Pathways is an online academic programme that supports homeschooling families. It is not a conventional physical school.

Your family continues to manage its homeschooling arrangements, while Valeem provides academic teaching, structure and guidance.

Who is this programme for?

It is designed mainly for families who have already chosen homeschooling and are looking for structured academic learning, experienced subject teachers and guidance towards future qualifications. That includes full-time homeschoolers, families balancing Hifz with academics, and parents who want a more personalised pace for their child.

At what age can my child join?

The programme has three indicative stages:

  • Explorers: 7–9 years
  • Innovators: 10–12 years
  • Scholars: 13–14 years

Children of around six and a half may also be considered, depending on learning readiness and academic placement.

What are the three learning stages?

Explorers develops academic foundations. Innovators strengthens subject knowledge and introduces broader areas of learning. Scholars focuses on academic maturity, subject specialisation and examination readiness.

Are classes conducted online?

Yes. The programme uses live interactive online classes, supported by relevant learning resources and Valeem's learning management system.

Who teaches the students?

Students learn from experienced subject educators from Valeem's established online teaching network. Teachers are allocated according to the programme, the subject and your child's academic requirements.

Do parents have to teach all the subjects themselves?

No. One aim of the programme is to provide subject-specific teaching, so you do not have to teach every academic subject on your own. Parents remain important partners in their child's learning.

Curriculum and examinations

Which curriculum does Valeem follow?

Valeem follows a British-curriculum-oriented academic approach, aimed at progression towards O Level and IGCSE preparation. Relevant coursebooks used in conventional schools are used where appropriate.

The specific subject plan and learning materials depend on your child's stage and academic needs.

Does Valeem follow Cambridge O Level and IGCSE?

The programme prepares students towards Cambridge O Level and IGCSE pathways through relevant subject learning and examination preparation.

Valeem is not a Cambridge school or an accredited examination centre.

Can homeschooled children appear for O Level examinations?

Homeschooled students may have private-candidate routes for eligible subjects, subject to the rules of the examination board and examination centre.

We help parents understand the pathway, subject options and relevant examination arrangements. Specific eligibility has to be checked for the student, the subject and the examination year.

At what age will my child appear for O Levels?

The intended pathway aims for suitable students to begin their first set of O Level examinations at around age 13. Remaining subjects may be planned across later sittings, typically around ages 14–15.

Readiness and subject preparation decide the actual timing.

Will my child have to take all O Level subjects at once?

Not necessarily. The intended model lets subjects be planned and examinations taken in stages over about two years, depending on readiness and the relevant examination requirements.

Does Valeem help with subject selection?

Yes. Academic counsellors guide parents and students by considering academic strengths, interests, future goals and subject requirements.

Does Valeem help with private-candidate registration?

We provide guidance to help parents understand the private-candidate examination process and the arrangements involved.

Actual registration, eligibility and acceptance by an examination centre depend on the applicable examination rules.

What happens after O Levels?

The intended progression is towards A Levels. Valeem aims to help parents and students plan the next academic stage, understand subject options and prepare for the transition.

Support, fees and joining

How does Valeem monitor student progress?

We use learning activities, recap sessions, learning resources and regular communication with parents to follow each student's development. Your counsellor will explain the specific reporting arrangements during your consultation.

Does Valeem provide parent counselling?

Yes. Parent consultation and counselling are part of the support model. The aim is to help you understand your child's academic progress, learning requirements and future academic pathway.

Does Valeem provide student counselling?

Yes. Academic and student counselling helps identify strengths, interests, learning needs and suitable subject choices.

Can children outside Pakistan join?

The programme's main focus is Pakistan, and we also welcome enquiries from families in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and other GCC countries.

Availability, scheduling, subject choices and examination arrangements are discussed during consultation.

How can I find out which programme is suitable for my child?

Submit the parent consultation form. A Valeem counsellor will contact you to understand your child's age, learning background, current homeschooling arrangement and academic goals, and then discuss the recommended stage.

What are the programme fees?

There is an enrolment fee of PKR 9,500 for all stages. Monthly fees are:

  • Explorers: PKR 11,000
  • Innovators: PKR 12,500
  • Scholars: PKR 14,000

Siblings get 10% off the monthly fee. Families outside Pakistan currently pay the same fees. Your counsellor confirms the right stage and timetable before you enrol.

How do I enrol?

Start by booking a parent consultation or sending an enquiry. The homeschool team will contact you, understand your requirements, discuss the appropriate learning stage and guide you through the next steps.
