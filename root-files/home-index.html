How we choose teachers

Every Valeem Teacher Passes Four Checks.

Fewer teachers make it through, so your child learns from someone who knows the subject and can teach it.

  1. ApplicationTeachers apply and share their subjects, levels and teaching background.
  2. Proven gradesThey must prove an A or B grade in the subject they teach, at O Level, IGCSE or A Level.
  3. One-to-one interviewOur team checks subject knowledge, academic depth and how clearly and confidently they teach.
  4. Onboarding and trainingTraining on managing student and parent expectations, using AI, presentations and running live classes.

FAQs

Questions Parents and Students Ask.

General

What is Valeem?

Valeem is an online education company, teaching live since 2019. It offers Online Tuition for IGCSE, O Level and A Level, Skills & Courses such as coding, IELTS, English and Quran, and Homeschool Pathways for homeschooled children aged 7 to 14. Over 650 vetted educators teach students in more than 14 countries.

Do you teach students in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf?

Yes. About 70% of our families live in the Middle East, most of them in Saudi Arabia. Classes are scheduled after school, in your time zone.

How are Valeem teachers selected?

Every teacher passes four checks: an application, proof of an A or B grade in the subject they teach, a one-to-one interview, and onboarding and training before they teach.

Online Tuition

Which levels does Valeem teach?

One-to-one live tuition for IGCSE, O Level and A Level students, usually from Grade 8 upwards. We also support younger students from Grade 5 with English, Maths and homework.

How much does online tuition cost?

Tuition is a monthly plan. The fee depends on the grade, the number of months, classes per week, class length and whether you want a fixed time slot. A counsellor shares the fee after the free demo.

How does payment work?

Once you are happy with the demo and the teacher, we send a monthly invoice and the plan renews each month. Many families pay from a Saudi or Pakistani bank account.

Skills & Homeschool

What skills courses do you offer?

Coding (Scratch for young learners; Python, SQL and Power BI for teens and adults), IELTS preparation, English improvement, language classes (English, Urdu, Arabic, Chinese and German) and Quran classes.

What is Homeschool Pathways?

A structured online programme for homeschooled children aged 7 to 14, with three learning stages and guidance towards O Level and A Level. Full details and fees are on the Homeschool Pathways page.

Getting started

Is there a free demo class?

Yes. Your child takes a free live demo at a time that suits you. If a teacher is not the right fit later, we arrange another.

How do I get started?

Fill in the form on this page or message us on WhatsApp at +92 332 2636574. A counsellor will call you, understand what you need and arrange a free demo.
