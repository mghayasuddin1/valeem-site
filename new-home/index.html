What is Valeem?

An Online School of Teachers, Built Around One Student at a Time.

Valeem is an education technology company that has delivered live online teaching since 2019. We recruit and train subject teachers, match them with students, and run the classes on our own learning platform so parents can follow progress.

What began as online tuition has grown into a network of 650+ trained educators teaching students in more than 14 countries, with our largest community of families in the Middle East.
